What this is

Penny Pop is a YouTube channel of short, bright animations about money: compound interest, credit cards, index funds, options, mortgages, taxes and retirement. Money in a Minute explains one idea in under sixty seconds; the deep dives take a whole topic from start to finish.

Every number on screen is calculated from the assumptions stated in each video's description. The videos are education, not financial advice.

The Penny Pop uploader

Penny Pop uses a small private tool, signed in with Google, to upload its own finished videos, captions and thumbnails to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel's owner and is not offered to anyone else. How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy.
